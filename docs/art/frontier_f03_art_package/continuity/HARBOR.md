# Harbor District Continuity

## Role

The Harbor is Frontier's working waterfront and the first major city district tied to fishing, shipping, boats, cargo, and coastal travel.

## Canonical city position

- Southwest waterfront of the main city.
- Market District lies inland / northeast of Harbor.
- Industrial waterfront lies farther east / southeast.
- Open water should remain visually prominent on the left, lower-left, or lower portion of the district composition depending on camera framing.

## Required visual anchors

Every approved Harbor image should preserve the identity of these anchors:

1. **Fishing Pier**
   - smaller working pier;
   - clearly distinct from heavy freight infrastructure;
   - room for fishing-related props and activity.

2. **Freight Terminal**
   - larger cargo-oriented structure;
   - warehouses, cranes, hoists, carts, containers/crates, or loading platforms;
   - visually heavier than the Fishing Pier.

3. **Boat Market / Boat Dealer**
   - moored boats or small sales/service dock;
   - should read as a commercial boat location rather than a generic marina.

4. **Fishing Store / Supply Building**
   - land-side building near the working docks;
   - connected naturally to Fishing Pier access.

5. **Harbor Contracts / Administration**
   - recognizable office or administrative building;
   - should look more organized/civic than surrounding sheds and warehouses.

6. **Harbor Entrance Landmark**
   - lighthouse, beacon, watch structure, or other navigation feature visible enough to help district recognition.

## Canonical sublocations

- Fishing Pier
- Boat Market
- Freight Terminal
- Fishing Store
- Harbor Contracts Office

Future sublocations may be added, but these should remain stable once implemented.

## Internal movement logic

The district should visually imply:

- Fishing Pier close to fishing supplies.
- Freight Terminal connected to a road leading inland.
- Boat Market directly accessible from water.
- Harbor Contracts positioned where both workers and merchants could plausibly reach it.
- Main inland route pointing toward Market District.

## Material language

Primary materials:

- weathered timber;
- stone quay walls;
- rope;
- canvas;
- painted metal;
- muted plaster;
- crates/barrels;
- practical dock equipment.

Palette:

- muted blue/green water;
- weathered brown wood;
- cream/stone buildings;
- restrained red/green/ochre accents;
- slate/iron hardware.

## Atmosphere

The Harbor should feel:

- busy but not chaotic;
- practical;
- inhabited;
- slightly salty/weathered;
- economically important;
- welcoming enough to function as an early-game district.

Avoid making it:

- a luxury marina;
- pirate-themed;
- medieval-fantasy;
- cyberpunk;
- derelict/post-collapse.

## Clickability and composition

Artwork should leave visually readable areas for:

- Fishing Pier hotspot;
- Freight Terminal hotspot;
- Boat Market hotspot;
- Fishing Store hotspot;
- Harbor Contracts hotspot.

Hotspots should correspond to real visual structures rather than arbitrary invisible menu areas.

## Flexible elements

These may change without violating continuity:

- number/type of small boats;
- crate/barrel placement;
- dock workers;
- gulls;
- nets;
- carts;
- decorative signage;
- minor sheds;
- flags;
- temporary cargo.

## Must not drift

- Harbor remains on the southwest waterfront.
- Water orientation remains consistent with the main city.
- Major inland road still leads toward Market.
- Freight and fishing areas remain visually distinct.
- Harbor entrance landmark remains recognizable.
- Permanent sublocations do not swap roles or positions arbitrarily between images.

## Day/night variants

Night Harbor should be derived from the approved daytime composition.

Potential overlays:

- warm window lights;
- dock lamps;
- lighthouse beacon;
- water reflections;
- subtle fog;
- sparse moving lanterns.

Geometry must remain stable for hotspot alignment.

## Future animation-friendly layers

Possible separate overlays:

- water shimmer;
- slow flag movement;
- chimney/smoke wisps;
- gulls;
- crane movement;
- lighthouse blink;
- small boat bobbing.

These should remain optional enhancement layers, not be baked into required gameplay state.
